Get to Know Us

Your Local Homies in the PNW

MyCleanHomie is a locally owned soft washing and pressure washing company serving beautiful Washington & Oregon since 2025.

We started MyCleanHomie because homeowners deserve a cleaning company that shows up on time, does the job right, and treats their property like their own. Every job is done by a uniformed technician — licensed, bonded, and insured — and every crew member on your roof is harnessed and tied off for safety.

We use EPA-approved treatments, satellite-measured estimates, and straightforward pricing. No upsells, no surprises — just clean homes and happy neighbors across Camas, Vancouver, Portland, and beyond.
